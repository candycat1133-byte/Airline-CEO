Archive Notes

Source Handling

The original kingdom names, leaders, stats, and lore ideas are preserved with light spelling and grammar cleanup. Chaotic lore remains intentionally chaotic.

Art Direction

This site uses CSS gradients, faction colors, badges, and custom fantasy symbols. It does not use official Minecraft assets, logos, or copyrighted art.

Expansion

Add more kingdoms in web/kingdoms-world/data.js. The cards, search, profile panels, and map controls will follow the same structure.
